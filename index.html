How to play

  1. Choose the number of teams. Each team puts a coloured token on START.
  2. Roll the dice and move forward.
  3. Read the card that matches the space you land on.
  4. Answer the question. If correct, you score a point and stay. If wrong, go back one space.
  5. Challenge cards are worth 2 points.
  6. A ladder square carries you up to a higher square. A snake square slides you down to a lower one. There is no card on those squares.
  7. The game ends when a team reaches FINISH. The team with the most points wins. A tie goes to the team that reached FINISH.
